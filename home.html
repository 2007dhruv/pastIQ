Curated Journey by Era & Dynasty

Explore how India's master builders sculpted rock, terracotta, marble, and sandstone across two millennia.

📍 Agra, UP Mughal

Taj Mahal

ताज महल
Built 1632–1653 CE • UNESCO World Heritage

White Makrana marble mausoleum embodying the pinnacle of Indo-Islamic symmetry, intricate pietre dure floral inlays, and quadripartite Charbagh gardens.

Explore in 3D
📍 Patan, Gujarat Solanki Dynasty

Rani ki Vav

रानी की वाव
Built 1063 CE • Maru-Gurjara Style

Subterranean seven-tiered stepwell built as an inverted temple on the banks of the Saraswati river, decorated with over 500 principal carved deities.

Open Dossier & 3D Archive
📍 Thanjavur, TN Chola Dynasty

Brihadeeswara Temple

बृहदीश्वर मन्दिर
Built 1010 CE • Dravidian Vimana

Commissioned by Rajaraja I, this granite titan features a 66-meter towering vimana capped by an 80-tonne monolithic stone cupola without binding mortar.

Explore in 3D
📍 Konark, Odisha Eastern Ganga

Konark Sun Temple

कोणार्क सूर्य मन्दिर
Built 1250 CE • Kalinga Architecture

Conceived as a colossal celestial chariot with 24 intricately carved stone sundial wheels pulled by seven galloping horses towards the rising sun.

Explore in 3D
📍 Hampi, Karnataka Vijayanagara

Vittala Temple

विट्ठल मन्दिर, हम्पी
15th Century CE • Vijayanagara Style

Renowned for its iconic monolithic stone chariot dedicated to Garuda and its musical pillars that resonate with distinct acoustic frequencies when tapped.

Explore in 3D
📍 Sanchi, MP Mauryan Empire

Great Stupa of Sanchi

सांची का महान स्तूप
3rd Century BCE • Buddhist Reliquary

Commissioned by Emperor Ashoka, the stupa encloses holy relics beneath a hemispherical dome, entered via four elaborately sculpted ornamental Toranas.

Explore in 3D
📍 Jodhpur, RJ Rathore Dynasty

Mehrangarh Fort

मेहरानगढ़ दुर्ग
Built 1459 CE • Rajput Military Fortress

Rising 400 feet above the Blue City upon sheer sandstone precipices, featuring seven colossal gates, intricate filigreed jharokhas, and royal courtyards.

Explore in 3D
📍 Ellora, MH Rashtrakuta Dynasty

Kailasa Temple

कैलाश मन्दिर, एलोरा
8th Century CE • Monolithic Rock-Cut

The world's largest monolithic rock excavation: an entire multi-story Dravidian temple complex carved top-down from a single basalt cliff face.

Explore in 3D
📍 New Delhi Delhi Sultanate

Qutub Minar

क़ुतुब मीनार
Built 1199–1220 CE • Fluted Minaret

A 72.5-meter tapering victory tower of red sandstone and marble, carved with delicate Quranic calligraphy and intricate projecting muqarnas balconies.

Explore in 3D
📍 Amritsar, Punjab Sikh Empire

Harmandir Sahib

स्वर्ण मन्दिर (हरिमन्दिर साहिब)
Founded 1581 CE • Sikh Sacred Shrine

Gilded central sanctum rising from the sacred Amrit Sarovar, featuring entrances on all four sides signifying universal equality and welcoming of all humankind.

Explore in 3D
📍 Kevadia, Gujarat Modern Era

Statue of Unity

स्टैच्यू ऑफ यूनिटी
Inaugurated 2018 CE • World's Tallest (182m)

Engineered along the Narmada River honoring Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel; a masterwork of modern civil engineering standing 182 meters tall with brass cladding.

Explore in 3D
📍 Mumbai, MH Indo-Saracenic

Gateway of India

गेटवे ऑफ़ इंडिया
Erected 1924 CE • Yellow Basalt Arch

Architect George Wittet's triumphal arch combining 16th-century Gujarati architectural ornamentation with European civic grandeur along Mumbai harbour.
